👋 I’m Ruben Verborgh, professor and
developer of decentralized Web tech.

  • I’m a researcher at:
    • KNoWS, IDLab, Ghent University – imec, Belgium 🇧🇪
    • with previous positions at:
      • Oxford Martin School, University of Oxford, UK 🇬🇧
      • Decentralized Information Group, MIT, USA 🇺🇸
  • I also work in industry:
    • Inrupt, USA 🇺🇸
  • I write blog posts, articles, books, … about the Web.

👋 I’m Ruben Taelman, researcher
on Web-based query technology.

  • I’m a senior researcher at:
    • KNoWS, IDLab, Ghent University – imec
  • My goal is to make data accessible for everyone
    through intelligent infrastructure and algorithms
    for data publication and retrieval.
  • Read more about me on my website.
